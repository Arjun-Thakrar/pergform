pERGform

Rowing-machine technique analysis on your iPhone and iPad.

Getting a good analysis

  • Put the phone on a stand, directly side-on to the erg, at about hip height, 3–4 m away.
  • Keep your whole body, the seat and the handle in frame.
  • Wear fitted clothing and avoid a bright window behind you.
  • Film 20–30 seconds. To see fatigue, film the end of a hard piece.

A catch or finish mark is in the wrong place

Tap the mark on the timeline. The video jumps to that frame. Use ◀ 1 frame / 1 frame ▶ to nudge it, drag it, or delete it, then tap Analyze again.

The analysis says it couldn't track me

Make sure your whole body is visible and well lit, and film from the side rather than at an angle.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Everything runs on your device. See the privacy policy.

Important

pERGform gives technique feedback for training. It is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat injuries. If rowing causes pain, stop and see a physiotherapist or doctor.
