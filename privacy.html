pERGform Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026 (updated October 12, 2026)

pERGform analyzes your rowing-machine technique from videos you record or choose. It was built to keep everything on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. pERGform has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not receive your videos, measurements or any other information about you.

Your videos

Videos you record in the app or choose from Photos are analyzed on your iPhone or iPad. They are never uploaded. pERGform doesn't copy them anywhere or keep them after you close a video.

pERGform Live

In pERGform Live, the camera picture is analyzed on your device as you row, one frame at a time, and then discarded. It is never recorded, saved or uploaded. Spoken cues are generated on your device. Live sessions are not added to your history.

Your history

When you analyze a video, the results (dates, stroke counts, angles, ratings and a small still image) are saved in the app's private storage on your device so you can see your progress. Like other app data, iOS includes this in your own iCloud or computer backups if you use them; we never receive it. You can delete one session or all of your history at any time in the app. Deleting the app deletes this history.

Camera, microphone and Photos

pERGform asks for the camera when you record, or when you start pERGform Live, so it can see you rowing. iOS also asks for the microphone because video recording includes sound; pERGform doesn't use the audio. When you choose a video, iOS gives pERGform only the video you pick.

Sharing

If you tap Share report, pERGform creates an image of your report and opens the iOS share sheet. Where it goes is up to you.

Children

pERGform doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
